What's Not Included

  • Ongoing content writing/copywriting (unless scoped)
  • Paid ad management / marketing campaigns
  • Graphic design/branding from scratch (can be coordinated)
  • Hosting/infrastructure costs (billed directly to client)

Why This Structure Works

  • Clarity: Clients know exactly what they're paying for at each tier.
  • Transparency: Framework choice (Next.js vs Vite) is justified by project type.
  • Scalability: Add-ons let clients scale up without renegotiating the whole scope.
  • Expectations: Timeline guide reduces scope-creep friction.
